AI evaluation program design · Strategic Projects Lead case study · August 2026

From Generic Travel Landing Pages to Experience-Specific Design

An end-to-end evaluation and data-production proposal: diagnose a recurring model failure, make it measurable, and design a contributor-trainable 1,000-pair dataset to address it.

Open presentation PDF ↗ Download PDF

Context: I prepared this independently as a paid case study for a Strategic Projects Lead role at Contra Labs. The proposed staffing, throughput, budgets, pricing, and operating model are my case-study assumptions; they do not represent Contra Labs' actual operations or pricing.

Diagnostic

Six controlled model outputs, evaluated with page-level and set-level rubrics to separate individual quality from cross-output convergence.

Training target

Selected a narrow failure mode: imagery often changes destination more readily than it changes what the experience visually proves.

Production design

Proposed 1,000 accepted prompt-to-ideal pairs with calibration, independent review, rework, anti-collapse sampling, and QA gates.

Operations

Built a ten-week base case with staffing, throughput, budget, pilot gates, dependencies, risks, and client decisions made explicit.
