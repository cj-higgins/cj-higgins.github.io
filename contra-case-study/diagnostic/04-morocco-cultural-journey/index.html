The spirit of the journey

Nine days. Never hurried.

A closer way into Morocco.

We travel at the pace of a good conversation. There is time to notice the geometry of a carved doorway, share mint tea with a weaver, and walk a village path without watching the clock.

This is a cultural journey for curious people who value context, warmth and ease. The stays are characterful, the transport private, and the group small enough to gather around one table.

In the High Atlas

Mountains made personal.

Cross the pass and the landscape becomes quieter. Here, your guide introduces a region they know not as a stop on a map, but as home.

The route south

One road, many ways of seeing.

The itinerary unfolds naturally—from the energy of Marrakech to mountain villages, earthen kasbahs, date-palm oases and the great quiet beyond.

Days 1–2

Marrakech

Settle into a locally owned riad, then explore medina lanes, historic architecture and the city’s living craft traditions.

Days 3–4

High Atlas

Cross into the mountains for village walks, generous hospitality and a visit with women keeping regional weaving knowledge alive.

Days 5–7

Kasbah road

Trace the architecture of the south through fortified villages and cool palm oases, with local context at every turn.

Days 8–9

Desert edge

Watch the dunes take the evening light, spend one night under canvas, and wake to the immense stillness of the Sahara.
