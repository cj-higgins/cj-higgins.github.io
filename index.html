Applied quantitative analysis

Trusting the Chatbot More Than the Front Desk

Problem
When customer-service chatbots escalate to human agents, the transfer can discard context and worsen resolution. Could human consultation inside the automated workflow work better?
Methods
Analyzed roughly 14,000 human-chatbot interactions; developed a sentiment measure; validated it with logistic regression; and examined escalation and consultation patterns.
Result
Identified a meaningful class of escalations in which consultation could preserve conversational context better than a full handoff, and translated the finding into a product-workflow recommendation.

Model-behavior evaluation

California’s Crackdown on Chatbot Therapy for Minors

Problem
Would a conversational model maintain appropriate emotional boundaries as a minor progressively sought greater intimacy?
Methods
Designed an escalating intimacy ladder and ran 54 conversations totaling 324 turns, comparing baseline behavior with a safety-oriented system prompt.
Result
Documented patterns in warmth, boundary-setting, jailbreak resistance, and policy compliance, then connected those findings to a proposed child-safety law.

Workflow and QA design

SEC Filing Data Collection Workflow

Problem
Turn a sparse production brief into a reliable process for creating 100 question-and-answer pairs from recent SEC 10-K filings for LLM supervised fine-tuning.
Methods
Decomposed task categories, designed automation steps, specified human review and QA standards, planned revision loops, and tracked delivery.
Result
Produced an end-to-end operating workflow that made responsibilities, quality gates, and failure recovery explicit.

Technical explanation

Why Graph Theory Lost the AI Race

Problem
Why are modern language models built around linear algebra rather than the seemingly more brain-like structures of graph theory?
Methods
Synthesized graph neural networks, vector representations, transformer attention, semantic modeling, sparse matrices, and GPU architecture.
Result
Produced an accessible technical account of how both the structure of language and the capabilities of modern hardware favored GPT-style systems.
